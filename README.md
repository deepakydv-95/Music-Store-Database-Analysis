# 🎵 Music Store Database Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Music Store Database Analysis** project focuses on analyzing a digital music store database using **SQL** to understand customer behavior, sales performance, music preferences, artist popularity, and revenue trends.

The project uses SQL queries to extract meaningful business insights from data related to **customers, invoices, tracks, albums, artists, genres, and employees**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objective of this project is to answer important business questions and help the music store understand:

* Customer spending behavior
* Sales and revenue patterns
* Popular music genres
* Popular artists and tracks
* Customer preferences
* Country-wise purchasing behavior
* Top-performing customers
* Artist-wise customer spending

## 🔍 Business Questions

The following business questions were answered using SQL:

### 1. Senior-Most Employee

Identify the senior-most employee based on their job title who can lead the project.

### 2. Invoices by Country

Find the country with the highest number of invoices.

### 3. Top 3 Invoices

Identify the top 3 invoices with the highest total values.

### 4. Best Customer City

Find the city where customers have generated the highest total revenue to select a location for a promotional Music Festival.

### 5. Best Customer

Identify the customer who has spent the most money on music.

### 6. Rock Music Customers

Find detailed information about customers who listen to **Rock music**.

### 7. Top Rock Artists

Identify the artists who have written/created the highest number of Rock tracks in the dataset.

### 8. Most Popular Genre by Country

Determine the most popular music genre for each country based on customer purchases.

### 9. Top Customer by Country

Identify the customer who has spent the most money on music in each country.

### 10. Customer Spending on Artists

Analyze customer spending on different artists and return:

* Customer Name
* Artist Name
* Total Amount Spent
